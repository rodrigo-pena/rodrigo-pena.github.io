## Platform

## Users

The site serves people who want a concise introduction to Rodrigo C. G. Pena and a reliable route to his professional profiles, work, and contact information. This includes academic peers, interdisciplinary collaborators, and other professional contacts.

## Product Purpose

This is Rodrigo's default landing page: a compact, durable explanation of who he is, what he does, and where to find more. It should provide enough context to orient a visitor while aggregating links to fuller professional and academic records.

Success means a visitor can quickly understand Rodrigo's current role and background, then continue to the most relevant profile, publication, code repository, supporting document, or contact channel.

## Positioning

Rodrigo is a computer science researcher and data scientist working in academia, with a focus on interdisciplinary collaboration. His current role is Team Lead Scientific Support at CeDA & sciCORE, University of Basel.

## Operating Context

The site is a public personal homepage rather than a comprehensive CV or publication database. Visitors should be able to scan it quickly and use its external links as authoritative paths to more detailed information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Present a concise first-person biography and current professional role.
- Aggregate professional, academic, publication, code, social, and contact links.
- Provide access to the academic genealogy PDF.
- Remain suitable for static deployment to GitHub Pages from the existing Astro project.
- Keep claims factual and maintainable; do not invent achievements, affiliations, testimonials, metrics, or other evidence.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Rodrigo C. G. Pena and preserve the pronunciation guide.
- Preserve the existing portrait.
- Keep the voice professional but not formal: concise, personal, and approachable.
- Preserve the first-person biography, academic background, profile links, and academic genealogy PDF as durable parts of the site.
