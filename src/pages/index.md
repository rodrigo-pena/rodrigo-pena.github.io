## Current work

<div class="text-group">

I am Team Lead in Scientific Support for [CeDA][ceda] & [sciCORE][scicore], at the [University of Basel][unibas]. As a computer science researcher and data scientist in academia, I work mainly on interdisciplinary collaborations.

</div>

## About me

<div class="text-group">

I grew up in Brasília, the [planned, modernist capital][brasilia] in the dry Brazilian highlands, and now live by the Rhine river in Switzerland.

I did my Ph.D. at [EPFL][epfl], where I investigated recovery algorithms for subsampled graph signals. My thesis is [freely available][thesis]. My doctoral studies were mostly funded by the European Union's _Marie Curie ITN_ [SpaRTaN][spartan]. You can check out my <a href="/downloads/academic%20genealogy%20rodrigo%20c%20g%20pena.pdf">academic genealogy</a>.

</div>
