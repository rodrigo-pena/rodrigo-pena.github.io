<div class="text-group">

I am Team Lead in Scientific Support for CeDA & sciCORE, at the University of Basel. As a computer science researcher and data scientist in academia, I work mainly on interdisciplinary collaborations. Browse [CeDA's projects][ceda] for examples.

## About me

<div class="text-group">

I grew up in Brasília, the [planned, modernist capital][brasilia] in the dry Brazilian highlands, and now live by the Rhine river in Switzerland.

I did my Ph.D. at the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne [(EPFL)][epfl], where I investigated recovery algorithms for subsampled graph signals. [My thesis](https://doi.org/10.5075/epfl-thesis-7219) is freely available.
My doctoral studies were mostly funded by the European Union's _Marie Curie ITN_ [SpaRTaN][spartan].
You can also view <a href="/downloads/academic%20genealogy%20rodrigo%20c%20g%20pena.pdf">my academic genealogy</a>.

</div>
